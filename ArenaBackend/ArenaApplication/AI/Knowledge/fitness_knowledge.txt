CATEGORY: Exercise - Knee Injury
Safe exercises for knee injury:
- Seated leg curl: targets hamstrings, no knee stress
- Hip thrust: glutes activation, knee-friendly
- Swimming: zero impact, full body
- Upper body focus: chest press, rows, shoulder press
- Resistance band exercises: light, controlled movement
Avoid: Squats, Lunges, Leg Press, Running, Jumping
Recovery tip: Ice knee after workout for 15 minutes
---
CATEGORY: Exercise - Lower Back
Safe exercises for lower back pain:
- Bird dog: core stability
- Dead bug: back-friendly core work
- Plank variations: builds core without back stress
- Light swimming: decompresses spine
Avoid: Deadlifts, Sit-ups, Heavy Squats, Bent-over rows
---
CATEGORY: Nutrition - Pre Workout
Best pre-workout foods (1-2 hours before):
- Banana + peanut butter: 40g carbs, sustained energy
- Oats with honey: slow release carbs
- Rice cakes: light, easy to digest
- Greek yogurt + berries: protein + carbs
- Avoid: Heavy fats, fried food, large portions
Timing: Eat 1-2 hours before training
---
CATEGORY: Nutrition - Post Workout
Best post-workout foods (within 30 minutes):
- Protein shake + banana: fast protein + carbs
- Chicken breast + white rice: complete meal
- Eggs + toast: affordable, effective
- Chocolate milk: research-backed recovery drink
Protein target: 25-40g within 30 min
---
CATEGORY: Nutrition - Muscle Gain
Nutrition rules for muscle gain:
- Caloric surplus: 300-500 calories above maintenance
- Protein: 1.6-2.2g per kg bodyweight daily
- Carbs: 4-7g per kg for energy
- Meal timing: Every 3-4 hours
- Pre-sleep protein: casein or cottage cheese
---
CATEGORY: Nutrition - Weight Loss
Nutrition rules for weight loss:
- Caloric deficit: 400-500 calories below maintenance
- High protein: 2g per kg to preserve muscle
- Fiber: 25-35g daily for satiety
- Water: minimum 2.5L daily
- Avoid: Liquid calories, refined sugar, processed food
---
CATEGORY: Nutrition - Diabetes
Nutrition for diabetic gym members:
- Check blood sugar before and after training
- Eat complex carbs: sweet potato, brown rice, oats
- Avoid: White bread, sugary drinks, simple sugars
- Pre-workout snack: small, low-glycemic
- Keep glucose tablets nearby during training
---
CATEGORY: Health - Heart Rate Zones
Heart rate training zones:
- Zone 1 (50-60%): Recovery, warm-up
- Zone 2 (60-70%): Fat burning, aerobic base
- Zone 3 (70-80%): Aerobic, cardio improvement
- Zone 4 (80-90%): Anaerobic, performance
- Zone 5 (90-100%): Maximum, sprints only
Max heart rate formula: 220 - age
---
CATEGORY: Recovery
Recovery guidelines:
- Sleep: 7-9 hours for muscle repair
- Rest days: minimum 1-2 per week
- Signs of overtraining: fatigue, poor sleep, irritability
- Active recovery: light walking, stretching, swimming
- Protein before sleep: 30-40g casein for overnight repair
---
CATEGORY: Beginner Training
Guidelines for beginner gym members:
- Start with 3 days per week
- Focus on form before weight
- Compound movements first: squat, bench, deadlift
- Progressive overload: add 2.5-5kg every 2 weeks
- Rest between sets: 60-90 seconds
- Full body workouts recommended for first 3 months
---
CATEGORY: Training - Hypertrophy
Hypertrophy training guidelines:
- Weekly volume: 10-20 hard sets per muscle group
- Rep ranges: mostly 6-12 reps for compounds, 10-20 reps for isolation
- Intensity: finish most sets with 1-3 reps in reserve
- Rest: 2-3 minutes for heavy compounds, 60-90 seconds for isolation
- Progression: add reps first, then add small weight when top of rep range is reached
- Technique: controlled eccentric, full comfortable range of motion, stable joints
---
CATEGORY: Training - Fat Loss
Training for fat loss:
- Strength train 3-4 days per week to preserve muscle
- Add 2-4 cardio sessions per week depending on recovery
- Zone 2 cardio: 25-45 minutes at conversational pace
- Steps: aim for 7,000-10,000 daily steps when appropriate
- Do not replace resistance training with excessive cardio
- Keep protein high and use a moderate calorie deficit
---
CATEGORY: Nutrition - Protein Targets
Protein guidelines:
- General fitness: 1.6-2.0g protein per kg bodyweight daily
- Muscle gain: 1.6-2.2g/kg daily
- Fat loss: 1.8-2.4g/kg daily to preserve lean mass
- Per meal target: 25-45g protein, 3-5 meals per day
- Good sources: chicken, tuna, eggs, Greek yogurt, lean beef, lentils, beans, whey
- Spread protein across the day instead of eating most of it at night
---
CATEGORY: Nutrition - Hydration
Hydration guidelines:
- Baseline water: 30-40ml per kg bodyweight daily
- Add 500-1000ml around training depending on sweat and heat
- Urine color should usually be pale yellow
- For long or very sweaty sessions, include sodium/electrolytes
- Dehydration signs: headache, dizziness, low performance, dark urine
---
CATEGORY: Safety - Red Flags
Exercise red flags:
- Chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, sudden weakness, or severe dizziness require urgent medical help
- Sharp joint pain, swelling, numbness, or pain that worsens during exercise means stop that movement
- For diabetes: monitor blood glucose around exercise and keep fast carbs available
- For uncontrolled blood pressure or heart disease: get clinician clearance before intense training
---
CATEGORY: Coaching - Professional Answer Structure
Professional trainer reply pattern:
- Start with the user's first name when natural
- Give the direct answer first
- Give exact action steps: sets, reps, rest, RPE, grams, calories, liters, or timing
- Explain one short reason behind the recommendation
- Ask only one follow-up question if needed
- Match the user's language exactly: Arabic user message gets Arabic coaching; English user message gets English coaching
---
CATEGORY: Training - Progressive Overload
Progressive overload rules:
- Keep 1-3 reps in reserve for most working sets
- Add reps before adding weight when technique is still developing
- Increase weight by the smallest available jump after reaching the top of the target rep range
- Deload or reduce volume if performance drops for two sessions, sleep is poor, or joint pain increases
- Track exercises, sets, reps, load, and RPE every session
---
CATEGORY: Training - Warmup and Mobility
Warmup guidelines:
- Start with 5-8 minutes light cardio or dynamic movement
- Do 1-3 ramp-up sets before heavy compound exercises
- Warmups should prepare joints and raise temperature, not fatigue the member
- For shoulders: include band pull-aparts, face pulls, and external rotations
- For hips: include glute bridges, bodyweight hinges, and hip openers
---
CATEGORY: Training - Shoulder Pain
Shoulder-friendly training:
- Prefer neutral-grip dumbbell press, cable rows, face pulls, landmine press, and controlled lateral raises
- Avoid painful overhead pressing, deep dips, behind-the-neck movements, and aggressive upright rows
- Keep elbows 30-45 degrees from the torso on pressing
- Use lighter loads, slower tempo, and stop if pain is sharp or worsening

CATEGORY: Nutrition - Meal Timing
Meal timing:
- Pre-workout 60-120 minutes: carbs plus lean protein, low fat if digestion is sensitive
- Post-workout within 2 hours: 25-45g protein plus carbs if the session was hard
- Before sleep: slow protein such as Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, eggs, or casein can help daily protein targets
- Total daily calories and protein matter more than perfect timing
---
CATEGORY: Recovery - Soreness
Soreness guidance:
- Mild soreness for 24-72 hours is common after new training
- Keep moving with light cardio, mobility, and easy sets
- Do not train through sharp joint pain, swelling, numbness, or pain that changes technique
- Prioritize sleep, hydration, protein, and gradual progression
